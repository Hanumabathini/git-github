## Fact-check (Nov 24, 2026)
The primary links in the brief (terafab.ai and the two investing.com/Reuters articles) were blocked from the session's network, so these claims were checked against the other reports cited.

| Claim in narration or brief | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Announced March 2026 | ✅ | Launch event on March 21–22, 2026 at the Seaholm Power Plant in Austin. |
| "Backed by Tesla and SpaceX" | ✅ (with a note) | The launch coverage describes a Tesla, SpaceX **and xAI** effort. The narration doesn't say "only", so it's accurate. |
| $16.8B initial investment, Grimes County, TX | ✅ | SpaceX's first phase. The state also awarded a $30M Texas Enterprise Fund grant, and the county approved a 35-year tax abatement. |
| Volume production targeted for Q3 2028 | ✅ | Said on the Oct 20, 2026 SpaceX call ("Q3 or Q4 of '28"). Musk called it "highly aspirational" and said it has "big error bars". |
| Chip uses: AI, vehicles, robotics, orbital computing | ✅ | Musk described inference chips for vehicles and Optimus, plus chips for orbital AI satellites. |
| Not yet producing chips | ✅ | First SRAM test chip at the Austin research fab is targeted for around mid-2027. |

Don't add any of these to the narration: the $20–25B headline costs from March, the 1 million wafers per month goal, or the 80% space-chip allocation. They are aspirational figures from different stages of the project, and sources disagree on them.
